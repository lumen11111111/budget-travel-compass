# BTC-006 Research Notes

## Commission

- Production title: **How to Choose Accommodation for a Group Trip**
- Primary query: `how to choose accommodation for a group trip`
- Tier / freshness: Tier A / HIGH
- Reader outcome: compare whole rental, multiple rooms, hostel/private-room, and hybrid configurations after room allocation, privacy, gathering, payment, and change costs are visible.
- Does not cover: obtaining group consensus (BTC-028) or settling expenses after the trip (BTC-031).

## Claim plan

1. Show why advertised capacity and bed count do not establish a workable group layout.
2. Compare configurations through use of space and responsibility, not a generic pros/cons list.
3. Calculate the stay commitment: accommodation, mandatory charges, deposits/holds, transport effects, and likely change exposure.
4. Verify occupancy, visitors, noise, access, payment schedule, cancellation, and modification rules for the exact listing/rate.
5. Avoid universal claims about hotels, hostels, rentals, or platforms.

## Source findings

- Airbnb's price model can include service, cleaning, extra-guest, pet, tax, and limited deposit components; payment splitting is conditional rather than assured.
- Airbnb requires mandatory reservation fees to be disclosed in platform pricing fields, with limited stated exceptions.
- Airbnb refund outcomes depend on the listing's cancellation policy and timing; property-collected fees may be treated separately.
- Vrbo describes a service fee and ties its refund to the reservation's full-refund window; this is a platform-specific example.
- The U.S. FTC fee rule covers short-term lodging and requires covered sellers to display total mandatory price up front. This is a U.S. disclosure rule, not a universal assumption about what a traveler will see elsewhere.

## Outline validation

The draft is organized as a group-space design exercise: draw the room plan, compare four configuration patterns, price the commitment, stress-test rules/changes, assign ownership, then complete a matrix. It does not reproduce BTC-028's group decision workflow.
