# BTC-007 Research Notes

## Commission

- Production title: **Hotel vs Hostel vs Vacation Rental: Which Stay Fits Your Trip?**
- Query: `hotel vs hostel vs vacation rental`
- Tier / freshness: Tier A / HIGH
- Outcome: a weighted decision based on total stay commitment, privacy, services, self-service workload, social environment, and trip pattern.
- Does not cover: individual property recommendations or deep group/access/remote-work verification.

## Claim plan

1. Prevent a nightly-rate-only comparison.
2. Describe operating differences as questions to verify, not universal category promises.
3. Match lodging type to the trip's daily pattern and failure tolerance.
4. Use current platform/regulatory examples only to demonstrate why checkout, cancellation, and payment details need verification.

## Source findings

- The U.S. FTC fee rule covers hotels, inns, short-term rentals, and vacation rentals and requires covered sellers to display mandatory total price up front.
- Airbnb's global total-price announcement says search results show fees before taxes, while its help page lists pricing components and notes location-specific taxes.
- Airbnb cancellation options vary by listing, stay length, booking date, and special cases; exact reservation terms control.
- Hostelworld's current first-party explanation distinguishes booking deposits and flexible-deposit credit, reinforcing that hostel booking-channel terms need separate reading.

## Structural decision

This is a “day in the trip” comparison: morning, daytime, evening, disruption, then weighted matrix. It is deliberately unlike BTC-006's room-plan exercise and BTC-008's evidence scorecard.
