## Primary research
- NOAA Climate.gov, “What's the Difference between Climate and Weather?” Updated 2024-04-12. https://www.climate.gov/maps-data/climate-data-primer/whats-difference-between-climate-and-weather
  - Supports weather as short-term atmospheric state and climate as long-term pattern; climate context cannot promise conditions on a travel date.
- NOAA NCEI, “WMO Climate Normals.” https://www.ncei.noaa.gov/products/wmo-climate-normals
  - Supports using climate normals as typical-condition context rather than a day-specific forecast.

## Freshness plan
For a real destination/date window, recheck current fares, official holidays/events, operator timetables, attraction/service dates, closures and transport seasonality. Article intentionally contains no live values.
