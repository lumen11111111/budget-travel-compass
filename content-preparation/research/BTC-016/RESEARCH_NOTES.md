# BTC-016 Research Notes

## Commission

- Title: **Plan a Microtrip Around a Concert, Festival, or Game**
- Query: `how to plan a trip around an event`
- Tier / freshness: Tier B / MEDIUM
- Boundary: the event is the anchor; this is not a generic three-day itinerary or ticket-buying guide.

## Claim and source plan

The draft uses only two freshness-sensitive examples: Ticketmaster's current event-status/refund workflow and Google Maps' planned-time routing. Both are framed as platform examples, never universal rules. Exact venue, promoter, ticket, transport, accommodation, and accessibility terms remain pre-booking checks.

## Outline validation

The narrative moves from the fixed event outward: define the anchor, choose a stay zone, protect the arrival buffer, add one before-and-after experience, build a cancellation fallback, then complete an Event-Anchor Worksheet. The opening is possibility-led rather than risk-led.
