# BTC-017 Research Notes

## Commission

- Title: **How to Explore a Destination Through Markets and Grocery Stores**
- Query: `grocery store tourism`; Tier B / MEDIUM.
- Editorial goal: affordable cultural observation with the batch's strongest travel-editorial personality.

## Research logic

Most of the article is observational and requires no citation. Strict claims are limited to allergen labeling and border declarations. FDA and U.S. CBP examples are explicitly jurisdiction-bound; readers are told to check local rules.

## Sources

1. FDA, “Food Allergies” — https://www.fda.gov/food/nutrition-food-labeling-and-critical-foods/food-allergies
2. U.S. CBP, “What food items can I bring into the United States?” — https://www.help.cbp.gov/s/article/Article-1470?language=en_US

## Outline validation

The draft follows a visit: enter with a question, read the store, compare staples, taste a small basket, behave as a customer, and decide what can cross a border. The utility supports rather than dominates the prose.
