# BTC-022 Research Notes

## Commission

- Production title: **How to Build a Carry-On Capsule Wardrobe**
- Primary query: `carry on capsule wardrobe for travel`
- Tier / freshness: Tier C / LOW
- Reader outcome: an outfit-compatible clothing system sized by activities, weather range, rewear tolerance, and laundry interval.
- Does not cover: airline baggage allowances, toiletry/liquid rules, or product recommendations.

## Claim plan

1. Start with conditions and activities, not a universal garment count.
2. Assign clothing functions: base, warmth, weather protection, activity/special use.
3. Use an outfit matrix to expose pieces that combine poorly.
4. Use a wear-count/laundry-cycle calculation to size repeatable basics.
5. Treat footwear and special-use items as high-volume exceptions that must earn their place.
6. Require a full test pack and at-home outfit test.

## Source findings

- The capsule method, outfit compatibility, and laundry-cycle logic are editorial planning tools and do not need decorative authority citations.
- National Weather Service guidance supports the necessary factual point that planning forecasts should be checked again as conditions change. The article therefore separates broad weather-range research from a final forecast check.
- Airline allowance is intentionally outside scope. The draft gives only one bounded instruction: verify the operating carrier and fare’s current dimensions/weight before test packing.

## Editorial design

This article uses a workshop structure rather than a general advice sequence. The Outfit Matrix and Laundry-Cycle calculation are completed in the middle; the final section is a physical test, not a motivational conclusion.
