# BTC-026 Research Notes

## Commission

- Title: **How to Use AI to Plan a Trip Without Trusting It Blindly**
- Query: `how to use AI for travel planning`; Tier A / HIGH.
- Boundary: decision method, not an app ranking or product tutorial; BTC-027 retains the first-international-trip workflow.

## Claim plan

AI may help turn constraints into questions, group notes, compare user-supplied options, and expose plan dependencies. It does not become the authority for entry rules, visas, schedules, opening hours, current prices, safety, or booking policy. Privacy behavior is product/account/setting dependent.

## Current first-party research

Google's Gemini Apps Help explicitly warns that responses can be inaccurate and should be double-checked. Its Privacy Hub, updated 15 July 2026 and rechecked on 17 August 2026, describes Keep Activity controls, default and selectable auto-delete periods, temporary/off-state retention, human review, connected-app effects, and longer retention for reviewed data. The draft uses this as one concrete example and instructs readers to inspect the chosen tool's own current terms.
