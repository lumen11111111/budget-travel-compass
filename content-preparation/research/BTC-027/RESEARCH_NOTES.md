# BTC-027 Research Notes

## Commission

- Production title: **How to Plan Your First International Trip Step by Step**
- Primary query: `how to plan first international trip`
- Tier / freshness: Tier A / HIGH
- Reader outcome: a dependency-ordered workflow that verifies entry feasibility before non-refundable commitments and leaves usable backups for arrival and emergencies.
- Does not cover: country-specific visa advice, a complete insurance comparison, a SIM/eSIM buyer’s guide, or exhaustive packing.

## Claim plan

1. State that entry requirements depend on nationality/passport, destination, transit route, purpose, duration, and personal circumstances.
2. Teach a source hierarchy: traveler’s passport authority; destination immigration/foreign ministry or embassy/consulate; transit-country authority; official carrier/airport only for operational confirmation.
3. Put passport/entry/transit feasibility before non-refundable booking.
4. Treat insurance as policy wording to compare against destination, duration, health, activities, cancellation, and exclusions—not as a generic checkbox.
5. Build resilient arrival, payment, connectivity, document, emergency, and trusted-contact plans.

## Source findings

- U.S. State Department and UK FCDO checklists both place destination-specific entry, passport, local-law, health, insurance, money, and document checks before travel.
- The U.S. checklist directs travelers to the foreign embassy for current visa/authorization requirements and recommends multiple document copies stored separately.
- The UK checklist emphasizes a backup method for accessing accommodation, flight, passport, contact, and insurance details if the phone fails, plus access to funds when the main method fails.
- UK insurance guidance says travelers should match coverage to destinations, duration, medical conditions, and planned activities and read exclusions. This is a verification framework, not a product recommendation.
- Government guidance is nationality-specific. The article uses government pages as examples of the correct source type, not as rules for every reader.

## Outline validation

The article keeps the commissioned sequence: feasibility; budget/dates; booking dependencies; arrival/money/connectivity/insurance; document and emergency backups; final 72-hour check. Visa, insurance, and connectivity remain bounded verification steps.
