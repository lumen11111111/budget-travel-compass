# BTC-029 Research Notes

## Commission

- Title: **How to Plan a Three-Day City Break**
- Query: `3 day city break itinerary planning`; Tier B / MEDIUM.
- Boundary: generic usable-hours method; BTC-045 remains the solo two-night framework and BTC-020 remains the one-base slow-travel method.

## Research logic

The draft avoids city-specific hours, fares, taxes, safety, and entry claims. Google Maps planned-time routing is cited only as a way to test route shape; the actual operator and attraction remain authoritative.

## Source

Google Maps Help, “Discover helpful features in Google Maps” — https://support.google.com/maps/answer/6291839?hl=en

## Outline validation

Convert dates to usable hours → choose stay zone → make geographic blocks → place reservations → build rain/fatigue swaps → run a final friction test. The utility is a three-day time-block template, not a Day 1/2/3 attraction list.
