## Primary research

- U.S. National Weather Service Safety portal: https://www.weather.gov/safety/
- NWS severe-weather preparation guidance: https://www.weather.gov/safety/thunderstorm-prepare
  - Supports regular official forecast checks and monitoring local warnings when severe weather is plausible.

## Freshness protocol
For a real trip, source the destination's official meteorological service, transport operator, land manager/attraction, and direct booking terms. Forecasts, warnings, closures, service status and change policies are retrieval-time evidence.

## Structural choice
Article is organized by information horizon and decision trigger rather than a generic checklist. Matrix focuses on planned anchor → dependency → trigger → alternative → deadline.
