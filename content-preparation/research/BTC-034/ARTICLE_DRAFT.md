# How to Build a Travel Sinking Fund

A travel sinking fund gives a future trip a defined target and a contribution rhythm. It does not decide whether the trip is wise, which financial product to use, or what percentage everyone should save. It answers a narrower planning question: given this target and this time, what recurring amount closes the gap?

## Define the funded target

Use BTC-035 or another trip budget to estimate the costs the fund will cover. Add a buffer only if you choose one, and state its amount rather than borrowing a universal percentage.

**Funded target = trip estimate + chosen buffer.**

Subtract money already assigned to this trip. Do not count hoped-for income or an unconfirmed refund as current balance.

## Choose target-date or open-ended

A target-date fund works backward from a booking or departure checkpoint. An open-ended fund uses a comfortable recurring contribution and lets the readiness date emerge. The first protects timing; the second protects contribution comfort. Neither is inherently superior.

## Calculate the cadence

**Required recurring contribution = (funded target − current trip balance) ÷ remaining contribution periods.**

**Fictional demonstration:** target 1,500 units, chosen buffer 150, current balance 450, twelve periods remaining. Funded target = 1,500 + 150 = 1,650. Remaining target = 1,650 − 450 = 1,200. Contribution = 1,200 ÷ 12 = 100 units per period.

These invented numbers are not a recommended budget, buffer or contribution.

## Recalculate instead of “catching up” blindly

After a missed contribution, update the current balance and remaining periods. If two periods pass and the balance is still 450, remaining periods become ten: 1,200 ÷ 10 = 120 units. If 120 is not workable, change the date, target or trip scope; the formula exposes the trade-off rather than judging it.

When the trip estimate changes, replace the target with current evidence and run the same steps. Keep the old estimate in the record so the change remains visible.

## Set a readiness checkpoint

Choose the moment when a booking is allowed: full funded target, the refundable booking amount, or another explicit threshold. The checkpoint must match the real cancellation and payment conditions; it is not permission to use credit or ignore other financial priorities.

Track the fund separately enough that the trip balance is legible, but this article does not recommend a bank or account type. It is general travel budgeting information, not personalized financial advice.
