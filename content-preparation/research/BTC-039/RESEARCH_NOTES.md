# BTC-039 Research Notes
- Brief: **How to Pack for a Long Trip Without Packing More**; `pack-long-trip-without-packing-more`; Packing & Gear / Packing Systems; Tier C / LOW.
- Promise: size a replenishable system by the longest interval between realistic resets, not by multiplying items by trip days.
- Boundaries: BTC-022 owns capsule compatibility and outfit combinations; BTC-024 owns layer functions; BTC-038 owns the cost of laundry and daily living.
- Utility: Laundry-Cycle Packing Calculator with climate-phase and specialty overlays.
- Planned links: BTC-022, BTC-024, BTC-038. No live-route assumption.

## Claim and source plan
No baggage allowance, restricted-item, textile-performance or destination-climate claim is made. The system is editorial and the arithmetic is fictional. Real carrier limits, care instructions, climate data and restricted items require current primary evidence.

## Arithmetic model
- Reset interval `R` = longest planned number of wear-days between realistic laundry/replenishment resets.
- Required functional units for a category = `ceil(R / verified wears per unit) + reserve units`, adjusted only for genuine simultaneous needs.
- Demonstration: R=6; base layers verified for 2 wears each; reserve=1 → ceil(6/2)+1=4.
- Quick-dry exercise tops: 3 sessions, one wash after session 2, 1 wear each → maximum inventory 2, not 3.
- Formula is a planning abstraction, not hygiene or textile-care advice.

## Structural choice
Draft follows phases and reset intervals rather than a list of universal item counts. This keeps Practical Utility distinct from BTC-022's outfit matrix.
