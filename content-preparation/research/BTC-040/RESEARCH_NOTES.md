# BTC-040 Research Notes
- Brief: **A Practical Reset Plan for a Road Trip Going Off Track**; `road-trip-reset-plan`; Travel Styles / Road Trips; Tier B / MEDIUM.
- Promise: an acute STOP → TRIAGE → SIMPLIFY → REPLAN sequence for a trip degraded by fatigue, delay, route, vehicle uncertainty, weather, conflict or schedule.
- Boundaries: BTC-036 owns cumulative burnout and pace redesign; BTC-023 owns comfort gear. This article gives no vehicle repair procedure or medical diagnosis.
- Utility: 20-minute reset card plus continue/pause/shorten/reroute/overnight/assistance decision tree.
- Planned links: BTC-036, BTC-023, BTC-030. No live-route assumption.

## Primary research
- NHTSA, “Drowsy Driving: Avoid Falling Asleep Behind the Wheel”: https://www.nhtsa.gov/risky-driving/drowsy-driving
  - Supports treating sleepiness while driving as a reason to pull over safely rather than pushing through; detailed caffeine advice omitted.
- FHWA, “About 511”: https://ops.fhwa.dot.gov/travelinfo/about/about511.htm
  - Supports using official current traveler information to choose time, mode or route in the U.S.; state implementation varies.
- NWS Weather Safety: https://www.weather.gov/safety/
  - Supports deferring to current official warning information.

## Risk controls
- No diagnosis, impairment test, repair steps, emergency maneuver or universal rest duration.
- Vehicle warning, unusual behavior, damage or uncertainty routes to the manual, roadside provider, rental company or qualified assistance.
- Emergency/unsafe location routes to local emergency authority; checklist never overrides instructions.

## Structural choice
The article is a short crisis protocol, not an itinerary template or wellness essay.
