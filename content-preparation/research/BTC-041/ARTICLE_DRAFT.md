# Arrival-Day Safety Plan for Solo Travelers

Before departure, write the arrival as a door-to-door chain: exact terminal, official transport boarding point, interchange, final walk or pickup, property entrance and check-in method. Save one supported fallback that still works after a delay.

The purpose is to reduce decisions when tired, offline or late, not to predict danger. Daylight does not guarantee safety and darkness does not prove danger; current route evidence and a clear check-in chain matter more than a citywide label.

## Build the chain door to door

Record the exact arrival terminal or station, border/baggage step, official transport boarding point, interchange, final stop, walk or pickup, accommodation entrance and check-in method. Estimate each link from the responsible source.

Airport and operator pages have different jobs. Heathrow, for example, publishes onward train, bus/coach, taxi and other transport information; TfL publishes journey/access options and status updates. A map pin alone does not replace either source.

## Prepare one supported fallback

Choose an alternative that remains usable after a delay: another official service, authorized pickup process, staffed waiting point or accommodation instruction. Save offline the address, booking name, phone numbers and route steps, while protecting sensitive personal data.

Confirm what late check-in requires with the exact property. Do not assume staff, a reception desk or a key process from the accommodation type.

## Use a calm contact plan

Tell a chosen contact the expected arrival window and what message means “checked in.” Agree when a missed message triggers a follow-up, then which official airport/operator/property contact comes next. Emergency services belong only at the threshold defined by the relevant local authority and situation.

## Escalation Checklist
1. Recheck delay and route status through official sources.
2. Contact the traveler/property/transport channel agreed for this situation.
3. Move to the supported fallback or staffed location.
4. Use local emergency help when the actual circumstances meet that threshold.

The card should make the traveler feel capable, not watched. BTC-042 covers complete first-solo readiness; this tool ends at a confirmed check-in.
