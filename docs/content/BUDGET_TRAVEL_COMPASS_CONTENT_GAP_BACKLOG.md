# Budget Travel Compass — Future Content Gap Backlog

Phase: Content Architecture Revision — Phase 2  
Purpose: preserve validated gaps without commissioning or writing them now.  
Rule: every item below is `BACKLOG_ONLY`; none belongs in the 44-article production count and none is ready for research, import, or publication.

## Prioritization model

- **P1 — architecture-critical:** closes a link or decision gap already referenced by multiple planned articles.
- **P2 — authority-building:** adds budget specificity, destination usefulness, or a missing practical workflow.
- **P3 — expansion:** valuable after the core clusters demonstrate quality and demand.
- A future item must pass keyword/intent research and source-feasibility review before it receives a content ID.

## Explicit exclusions from this phase

- No destination article is commissioned merely to increase article count.
- No current fare, hotel price, visa rule, schedule, safety ranking, or platform comparison is drafted here.
- No backlog item receives a production content ID or `READY_FOR_RESEARCH` status until a later approval gate.
- No body copy, CMS record, import file, D1/R2 asset, or site page is created from this backlog.
