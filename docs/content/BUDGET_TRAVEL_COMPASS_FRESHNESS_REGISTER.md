## Gate procedure

For each article entering controlled publication review:

1. Reopen every source named in its row and every HIGH-freshness ledger source.
2. Confirm responsible domain, page title, jurisdiction/traveler profile, update/access date and current claim language.
3. Replace unresolved source labels with URLs before import preparation; do not wait until publication.
4. Remove or narrow any claim that cannot be supported at the required authority level.
5. Recalculate any scenario whose real input changes.
6. Confirm the article still matches its metadata and internal-link promise.

No fixed “update every X months” promise is created. Rechecks are event- and publication-gate driven.
