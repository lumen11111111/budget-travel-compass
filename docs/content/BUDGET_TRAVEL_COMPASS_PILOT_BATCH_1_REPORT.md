## Common Writing Problems Found

1. **Discovery prices invite false equivalence.** Flight calendars make a number easy to compare before fare conditions and itinerary quality are normalized. BTC-013 now names “discovery price” and “usable total” separately.
2. **Official guidance can be nationality-specific.** A government checklist is authoritative for its intended traveler but cannot become a universal entry rule. BTC-027 and BTC-042 now teach a source hierarchy and state jurisdiction/nationality limits.
3. **Budget advice collapses into slogans.** A generic list does not answer the weekend-budget query. BTC-035 therefore centers a complete worksheet, cost behavior, scenarios, and a ceiling.
4. **Packing content defaults to universal item counts.** BTC-022 instead sizes the system by conditions, compatible outfits, acceptable rewear, and laundry interval.
5. **Solo-female guidance can become fear-based or prescriptive.** BTC-042 separates practical options from guarantees and responsibility, avoids destination rankings, and requires a sensitivity review.
6. **Cross-topic expansion is easy.** Each article needed explicit stop points so a pillar did not consume the planned sibling guide.

## Editorial Standard Adjustments Needed

The pilot supports the architecture, with these standards carried into future batches:

- Require a claim plan and source hierarchy before drafting every Tier A article.
- Keep a claim-to-source ledger with nationality/jurisdiction and “where used” fields; a bibliography alone is insufficient.
- Commission one complete practical tool per article and test its arithmetic, fields, or decision logic during content review.
- Label examples as current evidence, fictional demonstrations, or experience signals; never let one type impersonate another.
- Preserve a visible “does not cover” boundary in editorial QA for every pillar.
- Add a harm/sensitivity review whenever guidance concerns safety, gender, health, accessibility, law, or identity.
- Continue cross-article exact-paragraph, sentence, intro, conclusion, and H2-structure comparison after each batch.
- Recheck all high-freshness claims immediately before controlled publication; research completion is not publication approval.
